Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

Submit your request through the booking page, send the prepared WhatsApp message, and wait for LocxPert to discuss the details with you.

How much do services cost?

Pricing is determined during consultation and discussed before an appointment is confirmed.

Does LocxPert offer home service?

Yes. Home-service appointments are available across Lagos, subject to location and availability.

Can I consult before choosing a service?

Yes. Consultations can be arranged online or in person, by arrangement.

Can I send pictures of my locs?

Yes. You can select photographs on the booking form and attach them manually after WhatsApp opens.

What if I don’t know which service I need?

Choose Loc Consultation or explain your concerns in the booking form.

Can I request a particular date?

Yes, but your preferred date is subject to availability and must be confirmed by LocxPert.

Can I combine services?

You can discuss combining services during consultation. The final arrangement depends on suitability, time, and pricing.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Follow the preparation instructions agreed for your specific service. Mention concerns about your scalp, existing loc damage, or previous treatments before the appointment.
