Let’s Plan Your Loc-Care Appointment.

Tell us a little about your locs, the service you’re interested in, and when you’d like to book. We’ll review your request, discuss the details, and confirm availability with you.

Include your country code if you are outside Nigeria.

Choose “Not sure yet” if you’d like to discuss your options first.

Your requested date is subject to availability.

Home service is subject to location, availability, and travel considerations.

Share your locs’ current condition, goals, concerns, or preferred style.

Photos can help explain your locs and the look you have in mind. Choose up to 5 images, 5 MB each (JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC/HEIF). Files are not uploaded. Attach any selected photos manually after WhatsApp opens.

    Pricing is determined during consultation. Your requested date is not confirmed until LocxPert agrees on the service, price, date, time, and location with you.

    What happens next?

    A clear path to confirmation.

    1. Select the service you need, or choose consultation if you’re unsure.
    2. Provide your contact details, preferred appointment date, and service area.
    3. Add loc details and select photos if they help explain your request.
    4. Review the prepared WhatsApp message, send it, and wait for LocxPert to respond.
    5. Agree on the service, price, date, time, and location before the appointment is confirmed.

    Submitting an appointment request does not automatically confirm your booking. You must send the WhatsApp message, then wait for LocxPert to review and confirm the arrangements.
