Starter Locs

Beginning your loc journey? Discuss your preferred look, hair characteristics, and maintenance expectations so the right starting approach can be considered during consultation.

Keep your locs maintained with a routine tailored to their current condition and your styling preferences. Discuss retwist needs, maintenance goals, and suitable appointment intervals.

Loc Repairs

Address concerns such as thinning, unravelling, or damaged locs through an individual assessment. The appropriate repair approach depends on the condition of the affected locs.

Explore loc extensions when you want to discuss additional length or a different look. Suitability, method, and maintenance requirements should be assessed during consultation.

Discuss Extensions

Loc Washing and Conditioning

Give your loc-care routine focused attention with washing and conditioning options discussed according to your needs and the condition of your locs.

Styling and Updos

Explore loc styles for everyday wear, special occasions, or a fresh change in appearance. Discuss your preferred look and what is appropriate for your locs.
