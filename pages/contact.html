Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a LocxPert appointment?

Choose a service and submit a consultation request through the website. WhatsApp opens with your details prepared. Send the message and wait for confirmation.

How much do LocxPert services cost?

Pricing is determined during consultation. The service and its requirements are discussed before the appointment is confirmed.

Does LocxPert provide home service?

Yes. LocxPert offers home-service appointments across Lagos, subject to availability and travel considerations.

Can I consult before choosing a service?

Yes. Consultations can be arranged online or in person. Contact LocxPert to discuss the preferred format and arrangements.

Can I book for any date I choose?

You can request a preferred date, but the appointment is not confirmed until LocxPert verifies availability.

Can I send photographs of my locs?

Yes. You can select photographs on the booking form. If you do, attach them manually after WhatsApp opens. Photos may help with an initial discussion but may not replace an in-person assessment.

Which service should I choose?

If you’re unsure, choose Loc Consultation or describe what you need in the booking form. A clear photo may help explain your current locs and desired outcome.

Can I combine multiple services?

Tell us which services you have in mind when making your request. The combination, timing, and price must be discussed and agreed before the appointment is confirmed.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Follow the preparation instructions agreed for your specific service. Mention any concerns about your scalp, existing loc damage, or previous treatments before the appointment.

Appointment details, including availability, pricing, location, and any applicable booking conditions, will be discussed with you before your booking is confirmed. Please ensure that you understand and agree to the final arrangements before proceeding.
